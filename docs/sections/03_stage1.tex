\section{Stage 1: A0 (LLaMA-60M) reproduction}\label{sec:stage1}

Stage~1 is the gate experiment from the design document: ``coupled-vs-Muon gap must be real and $\geq 3\times$ seed-to-seed std before proceeding.'' We run all three optimizers under the dense LLaMA-60M baseline with five seeds per (optimizer, LR) cell.

\subsection{Where each optimizer's sweet spot sits}

The LR sensitivity is well-localised for every optimizer (Fig.~\ref{fig:stage1-lr-sens}). \optAdamW{}'s minimum is at $3\!\cdot\!10^{-3}$ (median final val loss $3.70$); the \optMuon-family pair share a best-LR of $10^{-2}$ (\optCoupled{} median $3.41$ over 5 converged seeds; \optMuon{} median $3.43$ over 3 converged seeds out of 5 attempted, the remaining 2 diverged). The \optMuon-family sees catastrophic divergence at $3\!\cdot\!10^{-2}$ (Coupled: 5/5, Muon: 4/4) and $10^{-1}$ (5/5 both arms), evidence that the $\mathrm{lr} \times \sqrt{\max(A,B)}$ scaling at $\mathrm{hidden}{=}512$ already saturates the operator-norm budget at $10^{-1}$.

The headline numerical result for Stage~1 is in Table~\ref{tab:stage1-summary} and Fig.~\ref{fig:stage1-forest}: \optCoupled{} beats \optMuon{} by $0.024 \pm 0.002$\nats{} on every paired seed at best LR, and beats \optAdamW{} by $0.293 \pm 0.025$\nats{}. The within-arm seed-std of \optCoupled{} at $\mathrm{lr}{=}10^{-2}$ is $\approx 0.003$\nats; the gap is therefore an $\approx 8\sigma$ signal at $n{=}5$, comfortably above the design document's $3\sigma$ gate.

\subsection{Coupling diagnostics on the bilinear products}

\optCoupled{}'s motivation is to flatten the condition number $\kappa(W_A W_B)$ of the bilinear products that drive the forward pass. Fig.~\ref{fig:stage1-kappa} traces $\kappa$ at layer 0 for the three coupled-pair categories, comparing one converged seed per optimizer. The Q--K and V--O products under \optCoupled{} sit roughly an order of magnitude below their \optMuon{} counterparts throughout training; the up--down product is closer to parity, consistent with the gate-omission caveat (Stage~1's MLP is SwiGLU, so the coupled pre-processor approximates the relevant Jacobian by dropping the gate term).

\paragraph{What would change this verdict.}
A 0.024\nats{} Coupled-over-Muon gap at $n{=}5$ paired seeds clears the design document's significance gate, but doesn't yet license a strong cross-optimizer claim. Doubling seeds would shrink the CI by $\sqrt{2}$; the \S d.4 ablations in Appendix~\ref{app:d4} go further by separately turning off the stage-2 polish pass (showing whether the gain depends on it) and the per-pair couplings (localising which of Q--K, V--O, up--down carries the signal).
