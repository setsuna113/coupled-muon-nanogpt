\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:concl}

The two stages of dense data assembled here support a tight, falsifiable claim: \optCoupled{} delivers a small ($\approx 0.01$--$0.024$\nats) but consistent loss-reduction over per-matrix \optMuon{} on 60M Common-Size dense architectures \emph{when} the multi-head Q--K coupled branch can leverage RoPE's 2-D rotation block. On the four RoPE-equipped rungs (A0 SwiGLU baseline, B GELU 2-mat, G SwiGLU + QK-Norm, H ReLU\textsuperscript{2} 2-mat + QK-Norm), paired-bootstrap 95\% CIs at each arm's best LR exclude zero and favour \optCoupled. On the two learned-positional rungs (C, D), the same algorithm regresses by 0.05--0.07\nats{} because the per-head Q--K branch silently falls back to a flat-2D coupling. Against \optAdamW, the entire \optMuon{}-family pair wins by $0.12$--$0.29\nats{}$ on every rung --- the cleanest, largest single effect in the study. Whether the dense-RoPE gap survives the sparse-MoE extension is answered in Part~II rather than asserted here.
